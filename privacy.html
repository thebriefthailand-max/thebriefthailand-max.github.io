Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

THE BRIEF is a Thai short-form news channel. "THE BRIEF Uploader" is our internal tool that publishes THE BRIEF's own videos to THE BRIEF's own accounts on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

2. What data the tool uses

The tool only accesses THE BRIEF's own accounts, after the account owner grants permission through each platform's official login. It uses:

  • Basic account information (account ID, display name) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos, captions and cover images that THE BRIEF created.

The tool does not read, collect, store, sell or share any data about other users, viewers or followers.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on THE BRIEF's own computer and are never shared with anyone. They are used only to publish THE BRIEF's videos.

4. Removing access

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in each platform's settings (for example TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

5. Contact

Contact THE BRIEF through our Facebook page.
